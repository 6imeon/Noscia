Noscia · neural ESG search

Five directions, five distinct palettes, each a clickable app.

Each direction now has its own muted palette and multiple views you can navigate — search, the entity table, corpus & index status, and BYOK settings. Same sample ESG query and corpus throughout. Open one and use its internal nav (sidebar, tabs, or 1–4 keys) to move between pages; the slim top bar jumps between the five directions.
