Noscia — logo exploration

Ten deliberately different directions for an ESG neural-search mark — from a literal search-lens-with-sprout, through monograms and abstract neural/graph marks, to a pure wordmark. Each shows the mark at logo size, a full lockup with the name, and a reversed version on a dark chip (to check it holds on both). Caramel = accent, sage = the ESG/leaf cue. Tell me the number(s) you like and I’ll refine + export SVG/favicon assets.
